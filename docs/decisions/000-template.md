# NNN. Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | rejected | superseded by NNN
- **Date proposed:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Date decided:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decided by:** the owner
- **Scope:** engine | policy | data | agent behavior | configuration

## Context

The measurable symptom or the question, with the numbers that raised it and where they come from (report, test, trace). No opinions here.

## Options

1. **Change nothing.** Expected effect and cost.
2. **Option A.** What changes, expected effect, cost, what it breaks.
3. **Option B.** …

## Owner questions

1. The question whose answer picks between the options.
2. …

Answers (owner, date):

1. …

## Decision

One paragraph, in the owner's words or approved by the owner.

## Acceptance

Which suites, tests, and reports show the change worked, and the numbers expected before it is called done.

## Consequences

What else changes (contracts, KB, docs, evals, costs); what becomes harder; what is now open.

## Evidence

Links to the reports and commits that informed and verified the decision.
